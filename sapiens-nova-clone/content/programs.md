**1. AI / Robotics** — видео workshops.mov (автоплей, фон bg-linen)
> The AI Robotics Programme is a fast-paced, hands-on programme designed for innovators, engineers, developers, and tech enthusiasts who want to build intelligent robotic systems in a collaborative, high-energy environment. Unlike traditional academic programmes, this bootcamp focuses on practical implementation, rapid prototyping, and real-world problem solving. Participants learn by building – working in teams to design, train, and deploy AI-powered robotic systems over an intensive short-term format.

**2. Personal Growth** — фото futurebydesign1.jpg (фон brand-azure)
> The Personal Growth Programme is a transformative course designed to help individuals gain deeper insight into their personality, strengths, motivations, and interpersonal style through structured psychometric assessment and guided reflection. At the heart of the programme is the use of the Myers-Briggs Type Indicator (MBTI), one of the world's most widely used personality frameworks. Participants explore how psychological preferences influence decision-making, communication style, stress responses, leadership approach, and personal development pathways. This course goes beyond labels. It provides practical tools for self-awareness, confidence building, emotional intelligence, and intentional growth.

**3. Start-up/VC** — видео winningteam.mp4 (фон brand-stromboli)
> The Start-up & Venture Capital Programme is an intensive, experience-driven course designed for aspiring founders, early-stage startups, and innovation-driven professionals seeking to understand the venture capital ecosystem while actively participating in it. This programme goes beyond classroom theory. It is structured around live pitching sessions, startup competitions, and professional sharing forums with experienced founders, venture capitalists, and industry leaders. Participants gain first-hand exposure to how investment decisions are made, how startups are evaluated, and what truly differentiates fundable ventures.

**4. Workshops** — видео hku_students.mov (фон brand-coral)
> This dynamic workshop series is designed to provide participants with immersive, practical experiences across four interconnected domains shaping today's global landscape: ESG and sustainability leadership, Japanese culture and business philosophy, and the evolving creative industries. Each workshop emphasizes experiential learning, collaborative exercises, case discussions, and real-world application rather than passive lectures. Participants gain practical tools, cross-cultural insights, and innovation-oriented mindsets applicable to both corporate and entrepreneurial settings.
