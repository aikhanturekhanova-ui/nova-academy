Official Programme

Imperial College London Motorsport Engineering Summer School

This official programme combines motorsport engineering fundamentals, practical design work, and direct exposure to Imperial College London's academic environment.

Explore Other Programmes

Programme Highlights

  • Build simple models; perform basic calculations; measure output and compare to predictions.
  • 1:9 scale racing-car workshop: investigate structure and driveline layout.
  • Powertrain design challenge: teams design a drivetrain for a F24 kit car.
  • Build session: assemble F24 kit cars according to the group design.
  • Introduction to motorsport aerodynamics: drag, downforce, pressure effects.

What's Included

  • All materials and equipment for the summer school activities
  • Stay at Imperial College London student accommodation
  • Engineering tour around Imperial College campus: wind-tunnel drives, lift drives, racing car powertrains
  • Certificate of completion from Imperial College London
  • Go through the University Application Processes by the College's Admission Team

Teaching Staffs

  • Dr Balazs Alexis Ihracska — Senior Teaching Fellow — Imperial College London

Learning Outcomes

  • Understand how mechanical systems transmit and transform power.
  • Explore the main sources of mechanical power and how engineers measure them.
  • Link power delivery to real vehicle performance and design a working drivetrain.
  • Understand how aerodynamic forces are generated and applied in motorsport.

Location & Venue

  • South Kensington Campus, London SW7 2AZ, United Kingdom
  • Located in the heart of South Kensington, one of London's most prestigious areas
  • Walking distance to Natural History Museum and Victoria & Albert Museum
  • Excellent transport links via South Kensington Underground Station

Payment Process

  • Applicant shall pay the remaining amount of the programme fee (within 10 days) upon acceptance of the offer.

Admission

To enrol in Imperial College's Programmes, applicants must demonstrate they meet the required level of English language competency level for the course. To assess the applicants' English language proficiency and overall suitability for this programme, applicants will be invited to attend an interview. Successful applicants will receive an offer email, which they can then accept (and also pay for the programme fee) to secure their place in the programme.
