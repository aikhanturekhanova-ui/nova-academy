Human+Tech Futures Summer Camp

Where future innovators learn to build technology that improves lives. Through hands-on learning in AI, sensing, and robotics, students will create impactful prototypes that address real-world challenges in health and well-being. Guided by experts, participants gain exposure to innovation, entrepreneurship, and global collaboration, preparing them for a rapidly evolving future.

Programme Highlights

  • Build AI Solutions for Real Human Impact
  • Master Human-Centred Design & Innovation Thinking
  • Learn How Startups Are Built — from Idea to Execution

  • Collaborate in High-Performing Global Teams

Why Participate

  • Learn from world-class experts: Led by a senior academic from Imperial College London and an experienced venture capital fund manager.
  • Work on real-world challenges with industry: Collaborate with teams from Pasona Group on real business and innovation projects.
  • Bridge technology and entrepreneurship: Gain both technical skills and startup thinking, rare in pre-university programmes.

What's Included

  • Accommodation at Hotel Bourou Seikaiha (shared twin rooms)
  • Daily meals throughout the programme
  • All teaching, workshops and materials
  • Local transportation in Japan
  • Cultural activities and site visits (including theme parks)

Learning Outcomes

  • A strong foundation in AI, innovation, and human-centred design
  • Hands-on experience building real-world tech solutions
  • Exposure to venture capital thinking and how startups are evaluated
  • Understanding of how to develop, validate, and pitch business ideas
  • Confidence in teamwork, communication, and presenting to an audience
  • Insight into real-world innovation through teamwork with Pasona Group

Location & Venue

  • Hotel Bourou Seikaiha, Awaji Island, Hyogo Prefecture, Japan

  • Modern conference facilities and dedicated spaces for hands-on learning
  • Traditional Japanese onsen (hot spring) experience for relaxation and well-being
  • Rich cultural surroundings with access to local attractions, dining, and theme parks

Payment Process

  • A deposit of HK$3,000 is required to secure your place.
  • Applicants who are successfully admitted must pay the full programme fee of HK$17,000 upon acceptance.
  • Early Bird rate — offer ends 29 Apr 2026 (HK$15,000).

Admission

To enrol in this programme, applicants must complete the application form. To assess the applicants' overall suitability, applicants may be invited to attend an interview. Successful applicants will receive an offer email, which they can then accept (and also pay for the programme fee) to secure their place.
