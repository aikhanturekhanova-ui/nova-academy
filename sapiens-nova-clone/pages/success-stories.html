Programmes That Changed Lives

A look back at the programmes, camps and hackathons we have delivered with our partners around the world since January 2025.

01 Bangkok, Thailand

Venture Building Programmes — Chulalongkorn University

Since January 2025, our engagement with the School of Integrated Innovation at Chulalongkorn University — Thailand's leading university — demonstrates our impactful work. We delivered two 15-week venture building programmes to 240+ first-year BAScii students.

  • 15-week core academic course delivered twice to 240+ students
  • AI coding, robotics and venture creation curriculum
  • Built robotic arms using 3D printers
  • Interactive learning: Sapiens Game & SuperNova Role Play

02 London, United Kingdom

2026 Summer Schools — Infosessions

Authorised by Imperial College London, SNA launched the inaugural Imperial Engineering Summer School for Under-18s in July 2026 — an intensive week of mechanical engineering through motorsport, hands-on lab workshops, F24 kit car racing and F1 guest speakers.

  • International cohort from HK, Singapore, Thailand and the UK
  • Top-tier instruction with Imperial Teaching Fellows
  • Meet Imperial Senior Admissions Officers
  • Imperial alumni networking plus a mini hackathon

03 Awaji Island, Japan

Human+Tech Futures Camp I & II

Camp I — Osaka University (February 2026)

SNA partnered with Pasona Group to launch the first three-day Tech Boot Camp in Awaji, Japan, for a group of students from the prestigious Osaka University.

Camp II — International Cohort (July 2026)

An expanded 5-day second edition welcomed elite students from Hong Kong, India and Thailand.

04 Hong Kong

HKU Summer Institute

NextGen Entrepreneurs Boot Camp

The HKU Summer Institute at the University of Hong Kong partnered with SNA to host a two-week NextGen Entrepreneurs Boot Camp in 2025. 100+ students from Germany, Russia, the US, the UK, Taiwan, Thailand, Singapore and China participated.

Technopreneurship: Launching Solutions through Innovation

Our co-founder Sammi Wong led two sessions at the HKU Summer Institute on "Pitching Skills & Storytelling" and "Fundraising 101 & Tech Startup Sharing" in 2026.

05 Hong Kong

Well Being — Future by Design

In partnership with Dr. Rina Lai, Cambridge psychologist, the Future by Design well-being programme at HKU Summer Institute 2025 focused on mental wellness, leadership, and storytelling in pitching.

06 Phuket, Thailand

British International School Phuket — Tech & AI Workshop

British International School Phuket (BISP), under the Arthit Ourairat Institution, partnered with SNA for a 2-day Tech and AI Workshop with 20+ Grade 12 students.

  • AI fundamentals, Generative AI and robotics applications
  • Team-based startup ideation and innovation
  • Final pitch presentations to school management
  • Built confidence in tech entrepreneurship and public speaking
