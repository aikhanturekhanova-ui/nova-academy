Our Success Stories

Programmes That Changed Lives

A look back at the programmes, camps and hackathons we have delivered with our partners around the world since January 2025.

01

Venture Building Programmes — BAScii Students

Bangkok, Thailand

Since January 2025, our engagement with the School of Integrated Innovation at Chulalongkorn University — Thailand's leading university — demonstrates our impactful work. We delivered two 15-week venture building programmes to 240+ first-year BAScii students. This core course covered venture creation essentials: AI coding, robotic arm construction (3D printers), idea generation, fundraising, evaluation and presentations.

  • 15-week core academic course delivered twice to 240+ students
  • AI coding, robotics and venture creation curriculum
  • Built robotic arms using 3D printers
  • Interactive learning: Sapiens Game & SuperNova Role Play
02

Imperial Engineering Summer School 2026

London, United Kingdom

Authorised by Imperial College London, SNA launched the inaugural Imperial Engineering Summer School for Under-18s in July 2026 — an intensive week of mechanical engineering through motorsport, hands-on lab workshops, F24 kit car racing and F1 guest speakers, in one of the world's leading universities.

  • International cohort from HK, Singapore, Thailand and the UK
  • Top-tier instruction with Imperial Teaching Fellows
  • Meet Imperial Senior Admissions Officers
  • Imperial alumni networking plus a mini hackathon

03

Human+Tech Futures Camp I & II

Awaji Island, Japan

Camp I — Osaka University (February 2026)

SNA partnered with Pasona Group to launch the first three-day Tech Boot Camp in Awaji, Japan, for a group of students from the prestigious Osaka University. Students developed practical and innovative venture ideas using AI and sensors to improve the operational performance of Pasona Group's hospitality facilities in Awaji.

Camp II — International Cohort (July 2026)

An expanded 5-day second edition welcomed elite students from Hong Kong, India and Thailand. Highlights: an elite cohort; industry focus on real-world AI, sensing and robotics in hospitality; venture creation and pitching to an international panel from 5 countries; and authentic Japanese cultural immersion.

Watch the programme video

04

HKU Summer Institute

Hong Kong

NextGen Entrepreneurs Boot Camp

The HKU Summer Institute at the University of Hong Kong partnered with SNA to host a two-week NextGen Entrepreneurs Boot Camp in 2025. 100+ students from Germany, Russia, the US, the UK, Taiwan, Thailand, Singapore and China participated. In teams, they explored Generative AI and Robotics, developed startup ideas and pitched them to three female judges, supported by 15+ guest speakers including venture investors, entrepreneurs, engineers and investment bankers.

Technopreneurship: Launching Solutions through Innovation

Our co-founder Sammi Wong, HKU's Honorary Lecturer, led two sessions at the HKU Summer Institute on "Pitching Skills & Storytelling" and "Fundraising 101 & Tech Startup Sharing" in 2026. 100+ students from China, Kazakhstan, North America, Southeast Asia, Taiwan and the UK formed teams to pitch startup concepts, receiving direct feedback from Professor Wong and two guest judges, alongside 4 guest speakers including venture investors and family office leaders.

05

British International School Phuket — Tech & AI Workshop

Phuket, Thailand

British International School Phuket (BISP), under the Arthit Ourairat Institution, partnered with SNA for a 2-day Tech and AI Workshop with 20+ Grade 12 students. In teams, they learned about AI, Generative AI and Robotics, developed startup ideas and pitched them to BISP's senior management.

  • AI fundamentals, Generative AI and robotics applications
  • Team-based startup ideation and innovation
  • Final pitch presentations to school management
  • Built confidence in tech entrepreneurship and public speaking
06

Tech Hackathons in Saudi Arabia

Riyadh, Saudi Arabia

NextG3N Web3 Hackathon

Co-organised with the Munich-based W3-FF Venture Builder, SNA participated in the NextG3N Web3 Hackathon at Princess Nourah Bint Abdulrahman University in Riyadh. Over three days, it attracted 100+ participants from 16 nationalities, with women making up over 40% of attendees. The hackathon featured 8 hacking teams and 14 sponsors, with strong support from HRH Princess Nourah AlFaisal of Saudi Arabia.

Trust & Pay Hackathon

In February 2026, SNA co-organised the Trust & Pay Hackathon with the Munich-based W3-FF Venture Builder at Prince Saud University in Riyadh. Over three days, nine teams from across the Middle East participated in this dynamic fintech event, including college students and working professionals. We were honoured to have Bosch, the German tech giant, as principal sponsor, alongside more than 15 supporting organisations, including the Web3 Alliance of Saudi Arabia and the Fetch.ai Foundation.
