About the Institute

Research. Hands-On Experience. Venture Building.

SNA Institute, part of SNA, bridges the gap between academic research and practical application. The Institute conducts research in AI, Robotics and IoT, and provides a platform for exceptional graduates of our programmes to gain intensive, hands-on experience through our Elite Internship Programme. This internship encompasses both academic research in AI, Robotics and IoT, as well as valuable venture building experience.

  • 01

    Research in AI, Robotics & IoT

    Conducting applied research at the intersection of artificial intelligence, robotics and the Internet of Things — turning ideas into working prototypes.

  • 02

    Elite Internship Programme

    A platform for exceptional graduates of our programmes to gain intensive, hands-on experience guided by our mentors in residence.

  • 03

    Venture Building Experience

    Beyond research — interns develop the entrepreneurial mindset to build ventures that bring technology to the world.
