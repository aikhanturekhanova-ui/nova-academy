Who We Are

Sapiens Nova Academy Limited(SNA), recognizes that well-being,art and culture, venture building, and innovation are interconnected components essential for cultivating well-rounded young generations. Well-being creates a strong foundation for mental resilience, enabling creativity and fostering a positive mindset. Art and Culture stimulate creative thinking, which is vital in venture building/entrepreneurship

  • 01

    Venture-Building

    Our venture building programmes focus on innovation, entrepreneurship, and technology venture creation and investment. We deliver customised programmes in Hong Kong, Thailand, Japan, China, and the UK, combining boot camps, hackathons, pitching sessions, workshops, professional sharing events, and start-up or VC competitions to provide global exposure and hands-on experience for impactful ventures.

  • 02

    Well-Being

    In partnership with Dr. Rina Lai, Cambridge psychologist, we deliver well-being programmes that foster mental wellness, leadership, and storytelling in pitching. Through focused workshops, students build resilience and a growth mindset, highlighting the importance of wellness and communication for success.

  • 03

    Art & Culture

    Collaborated with Pasona Group Inc. (2168.T), a leading Tokyo Stock Exchange company. Through this collaboration, students gain access to Awaji Island's premier facilities—music theatre, luxury hotels, theme parks, and fine dining—providing an ideal stage for performances and cultural exchange with Japanese artists.
